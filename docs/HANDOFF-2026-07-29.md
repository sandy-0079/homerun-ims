writing empty state, and the re-upload restored it.

before anything is stored. Both call sites catch it. 257 tests.

Backups re-taken and verified identical to live: `invoice_data_backup_20260729` (74,381 rows) and
`catalogue_backup_20260729` (skuMaster 2,092 / priceData 1,833).
